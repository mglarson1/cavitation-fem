## Scientific qualifications

Passing the regression does not mean every method in Table 8 converges:
two full-gradient active-set rows intentionally record failure at the
iteration cap. Their reported numbers are last-iterate diagnostics.
The QP references instead satisfy explicit finite-precision optimality
checks. Small central-path products remain unresolved in relative terms.

The old Table 2 averaged-strip experiment is superseded by raw 2D errors.
The current data support first-order gradient accuracy; the observed L2
rate is not a general theorem. End-location and front-refinement tests
retain their stated meshes, observation windows and sampling rules.
The pressure-compatible numerical boundary variant is not silently covered
by the original Stokes boundary analysis. Grid-alignment and domain-end
sensitivity remain limitations of the cavity comparison.
